# Editorial branch

Use this branch only when the concept or caption is mutable.

## Direction contract

Write a private one-line brief with exactly one item in each field:

- recognition hinge: the smallest exact quote, prop, action, reaction, number, relationship, or plot mechanic that identifies the scene;
- AI bridge: one concrete AI or AI-safety concept;
- realization: why the visible scene literally demonstrates that bridge;
- must-preserve regions: the visual evidence the final image cannot hide.

Generate genuinely different directions by changing the hinge, comic ingredient, or native format. Do not produce paraphrases of one joke. Reject any direction that needs plot-summary prose or a second abstraction to land.

## Selection priors

- Recognition should arrive before abstraction.
- Prefer a canonical line, precise technical relabel, collision, character exchange, real state contrast, or genuinely source-native interface.
- Use technical language only when it precisely renames what the viewer can see.
- Specific profanity, emotion, bluntness, or weirdness can beat a sanitized explanation when it belongs to the source.
- Favor one forceful beat when the image supplies the setup and two concise beats for a real setup/payoff, inversion, contrast, or exchange.
- Stop after the payoff. Do not add a taxonomy label, moral, caveat, or explanatory footer.

Avoid generic dashboards, status boilerplate, invented explanatory dialogue, scene-wide ontology labels, competing concepts, and a third beat after the joke has ended.

## Copy provenance

Mark each semantic line as one of:

- `canonical-quote` only when supplied or verified exactly;
- `intentional-rewrite` when it is user-supplied or knowingly echoes source grammar;
- `original` for new copy.

Do not put uncertain dialogue in quotation marks. Preserve names, numbers, units, code, cadence, and punctuation with meaning. Generated fragments omit cosmetic terminal periods. Questions, exclamations, decimals, ellipses, abbreviations, filenames, and locked or canonical copy retain their punctuation.
