# Taste

Distilled from the owner's ratings and notes across review rounds 1–5 (203 finalized memes). The round files under `data/meme-review/rounds/` hold the evidence; this is the working summary. Add a line here when a pick or rejection teaches something reusable.

## What gets finalized

One unmistakable scene cue plus one concrete AI bridge, then stop.

- Two caption lines in 181 of 203 finalized memes; median 9 words, rarely more than 12.
- Text over the authentic still, cover-framed, top and bottom (191 overlay, 199 cover). Bands and letterboxing almost never survive.
- Formats by count: collision 94, relabel 48, canon 29, source-native 12, state contrast 11, dialogue 9.
- Recognition leads. The canonical phrase or number goes first and the AI mapping rides on context.
- `WHEN …` grammar works when the collision is real; it is not a default template.
- A contemporary swap works as one surgical noun inside source-native grammar, never as a topical paragraph.
- Precise technical weirdness beats generic AI vocabulary.

## What gets rejected

- A second line that does not hit after a strong first line. Keep the first and rebuild the second.
- Code, logs, and pseudocode captions: “too much to parse.”
- Cute but obvious: recognition without a payoff lands at neutral.
- Text over the subject's face or the recognition hinge.
- Dashboards, status labels, taxonomy terms, explanatory dialogue, or an AI label stapled onto a plot summary.
- Small type. When in doubt, make it larger and cut words.

A loved frame with weak copy is still a good frame: rewrite the copy, keep the image.
