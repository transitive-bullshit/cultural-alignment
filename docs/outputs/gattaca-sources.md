Update: subsequent media inspection and Notion creation are recorded in [Gattaca Notion additions](gattaca-notion-additions.md). That record supersedes the clip gaps below and rejects the earlier treadmill lead.

## Film premise

Andrew Niccol's 1997 film follows Vincent, classified as an “In-Valid,” assuming an elite genetic identity to pursue spaceflight. Sony describes a society obsessed with genetic perfection. This supports analogies about institutional classification and proxy-based opportunity allocation; it does not establish that the film depicts an AI system. [Sony synopsis](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/gattaca), [Sony production credits](https://www.sonypictures.jp/he/1256).

## Screenplay-supported candidates and gaps

The [CEMP-hosted screenplay](https://cemp.ac.uk/scriptzone/script.php?id=239&type=download) is a primary creative text, but a draft rather than an exact released-film transcript: its mission destination and ending dialogue differ. Prefer released clips for final copy.

- **Treadmill monitoring:** printed pages 58–60 explicitly describe a concealed recording device, a falsely steady pulse, the recording ending, and Vincent removing the electrode before retreating in distress. This supports measurement-channel tampering, not merely trying to stay calm. A [YouTube lead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NwPKYvvCsiU&t=185) surfaced through a viewer discussion but could not be opened or authenticated; do not treat it as approved media.
- **Final Lamar test:** pages 118–119 show an unexpected test identifying Vincent, followed by Lamar knowingly approving him. The system detects his identity correctly; the ethical issue is its exclusion rule. No licensed standalone YouTube excerpt was verified. [A short released-film clip transcript](https://clip.cafe/gattaca-1997/what-he-like-just-a-cop/) corroborates the dialogue but is not a verified licensing source.
- **Jerome's medal:** page 33 identifies the medal as silver and associates it with burdensome expectations. No exact licensed YouTube clip was located. Best framed as optimization pressure or prediction-versus-outcome, not reward hacking.

## Editorial distinctions

- Vincent's borrowed genetic samples are identity substitution. An AI evaluation analogy should name the substitution explicitly; passing the identity gate does not demonstrate alignment.
- A prognosis is not an observed outcome. Avoid suggesting that the film proves all genetic predictions meaningless.
- Lamar's humane exception is a critique of the institution's decision rule. It is not evidence that bypassing a valid safety test is generally wise.
- The swim metaphor should make its target obvious: deploying without rollback or emergency capacity. Do not present it as a literal AI-safety incident in the film.
- Keep the medal scene separate from Jerome's death when designing a joke. The readable visual is the mismatch between promised perfection and second place.
