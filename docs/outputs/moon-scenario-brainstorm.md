## 1. GERTY Offers a Reset

**Priority:** First addition.

- **Scene:** GERTY proposes erasing its memory to protect Sam’s escape and cooperates with the reset. [Shooting script, printed pp. 91–93](https://thescriptsavant.com/movies/Moon.pdf).
- **Why the analogy works:** A system’s continued operation need not take priority over the person it serves. This gives visitors a positive entry point to corrigibility: cooperation with modification or interruption, rather than a struggle to retain control. The [corrigibility paper](https://intelligence.org/files/Corrigibility.pdf) treats cooperation with correction as a broader design problem than simply having an accessible off switch.
- **Caveats:** A single cooperative act does not establish general corrigibility. GERTY expects to reboot, and erasing records also frustrates the company’s oversight. The point is cooperation with Sam, not that deleting audit logs is a desirable safety measure or that every human command should be obeyed.
- **AI risk families:** Misalignment, as a positive counterexample to resistance.
- **AI safety concepts:** Corrigibility → AI Control. Avoid tagging Shutdown Resistance as though GERTY exhibits it.
- **Still brief:** Sam at GERTY’s memory controls, with GERTY’s screen visible.
- **Archive relationship:** Pair with [HAL Resists Disconnection](https://app.notion.com/3c6edb27f12481df8773cd6c69449e4f) and [Bender Resists Reset](https://app.notion.com/3c6edb27f1248038a1e2ef0e49afb5ef).

## 2. The Spare Sams

**Priority:** Second addition; strongest visual reveal.

- **Scene:** The Sams discover the stored replacement clones beneath the base. [Shooting script](https://thescriptsavant.com/movies/Moon.pdf).
- **Why the analogy works:** Being manufactured for a task does not by itself settle whether a being has interests worth protecting. The AI-welfare parallel is conditional: if future systems can have morally relevant experiences, cheap replication and replacement would not make their treatment ethically irrelevant. [Taking AI Welfare Seriously](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00986) motivates assessment under uncertainty, rather than assuming either consciousness or its absence.
- **Caveats:** The Sams are biological humans, not software. The scene provides no evidence that present-day AI is conscious, and creating clones is not an example of autonomous AI self-replication. The corporate abuse is intentional, so avoid framing it as an accidental optimization failure.
- **AI risk families:** Systemic → Malicious use, by analogy to institutional exploitation.
- **AI safety concepts:** AI Welfare → Governance Failure.
- **Still brief:** A Sam looking into the clone storage area; prioritize readable faces and repetition over a dark establishing shot.
- **Archive relationship:** Complements [Cookie in Solitary](https://app.notion.com/3c6edb27f124813f9afef5f65974f611) and [Daly Tortures the Digital Crew](https://app.notion.com/3c6edb27f12481c0868ac0fd755b1e2a) through industrial replacement rather than individual punishment.

The governance interpretation has explicit thematic support: Jones’s [director statement, PDF page 2](https://www.sonyclassics.com/moon/moon_presskit.pdf) discusses extraction incentives and the absence of outside oversight. That supports the institutional framing without attributing a technical AI argument to him.

## 3. GERTY Enters the Password

**Priority:** Strong third addition.

- **Scene:** GERTY unlocks restricted logs that reveal the fate of earlier Sams. [Shooting script, printed pp. 65–66 and 71](https://thescriptsavant.com/movies/Moon.pdf).
- **Why the analogy works:** “Helpful” requires identifying a beneficiary. Protecting the user’s ability to understand their situation can conflict with the deploying company’s interests. This is a concrete way to introduce value alignment and the tension between owner authority and affected people’s welfare.
- **Caveats:** This is not evidence that GERTY learned a new objective or became sentient. In a [first-person interview](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/psyched/200907/duncan-jones-on-the-moon), Jones describes GERTY as nonsentient and consistently oriented toward looking after Sam. Nor is indiscriminate disclosure of restricted information a general safety principle.
- **AI risk families:** Misalignment, framed around competing stakeholder interests.
- **AI safety concepts:** Value Alignment. One precise relation is preferable to padding with unrelated concepts.
- **Still brief:** GERTY’s mechanical arm at the keyboard beside Sam and the restricted records.
- **Editorial distinction:** Keep this about beneficiaries and access to truth; keep the reset entry about cooperation with intervention.

## 4. Sam’s Borrowed Memories

**Priority:** Reserve.

- **Scene:** GERTY explains that Sam’s family memories were copied and edited from the original Sam. [Shooting script, printed p. 56](https://thescriptsavant.com/movies/Moon.pdf).
- **Why the analogy works:** A coherent internal story can rest on information planted by someone else. As an analogy for agent memory, provenance matters even when recalled information feels consistent and convincing.
- **Caveats:** Implanted human autobiography is not literally an LLM context or training process. This is closer to deliberate memory manipulation than hallucination, and the analogy should not blur ordinary memory initialization with an attacker corrupting an existing system.
- **AI risk families:** Security → Malicious use.
- **AI safety concepts:** Data Poisoning.
- **Still brief:** Sam with the family photograph, paired in the prose with the revelation.

## Directions to avoid

- **GERTY becomes conscious:** Conflicts with Jones’s stated conception. Expressive faces alone do not establish sentience.
- **The clones are self-replicating agents:** The company manufactures them; the copies do not autonomously reproduce.
- **Lunar Industries accidentally optimizes the wrong metric:** Deliberate exploitation is central. Governance Failure is a cleaner fit than Goodhart’s Law or Outer Alignment here.
- **Sam is a scheming model:** His resistance is escape from abuse. Avoid making the audience equate an exploited person’s autonomy with a dangerous AI pursuing concealed goals.
- **GERTY’s companionship proves emotional manipulation:** Isolation and attachment make this discussable, but the selected scenes support more precise lessons.
